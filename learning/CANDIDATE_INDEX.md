### Retrieval and confidence aren't the same

- **Attention:** An existing candidate may make a related event easier to notice.
- **Evidence strength:** Depends on relevance, quality, discriminating power, and independence—not on being listed here.
- **Priority:** Depends on potential benefit, cost, risk, and human authorization.

The index is a *cue for inquiry*, not a filter on what observations count. Novel evidence, contrary examples, and a justified decision to make no change are equally valid outcomes. Consult the [reconciliation protocol](EVIDENCE_RECONCILIATION.md) when an observation merits durable attention.
