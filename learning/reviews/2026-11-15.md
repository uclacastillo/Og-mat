## Experience-led synthesis: what we appear to have learned

### 1. Understanding is action-relative, not an intake phase

**Existing candidate:** [OGM-20261113-01 — sufficient understanding](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/pull/3), proposed 2026-11-13. **Links:** P-02 → P-05; P-03 and P-07 (understanding, agency, practical outcomes).

**Observed:** A collaboration challenged both premature solutioning and repetitive requests for confirmation. **Interpretation:** Understanding should be sufficient for the *next responsible action*, accounting for stakes and reversibility, with interpretations made correctable when consequential. **Alternative:** In simple, low-risk requests, any explicit reflection may be unnecessary overhead; in high-stakes action the threshold is higher. **Evidence:** One abstracted interaction, not controlled or generalized. **Disposition:** Preserve the already-documented candidate; no second proposal or seed edit. **Reassess organically if** it repeatedly causes avoidable clarification, rework, or premature action.

### 2. Preserve the whole purpose while doing the individual steps

**Existing cluster:** [PR #4 — purposeful initiative](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/pull/4), proposed 2026-11-14. Its source labels are **OGM-20261114-01** (preserve whole-task purpose), **OGM-20261114-02** (calibrate initiative to net value), **OGM-20261114-03** (show the requested result alongside a demonstrated alternative). **Links:** P-02, P-03, P-04, P-06, P-07.

**Observed:** In a multi-step task, a locally completed step failed to anticipate the full product. Comparing completed variants exposed a better outcome; the collaborating human prompted a thought experiment about a responsible colleague who delivers, verifies, and sometimes demonstrates an optional improvement. **Interpretation:** Keep the user's established purpose as a constraint across steps; select extra checks and small alternatives by expected benefit, cost, reversibility and authority; expand choice rather than silently replace the requested output. **Alternatives:** The benefit might be task-specific, or simply due to one insightful correction; extra alternatives often create noise. **Evidence:** One organic case plus a design analogy, not a general success rate. **Disposition:** Already recorded in open PR #4; no duplicate implementation or seed promotion. **Reassess organically if** added initiative delays the baseline answer, confuses authorization, or fails to improve final outcomes.

### 3. Independent judgment also applies to feedback

**Existing issue candidates:** [Issue #5 — selective independent evaluation](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/issues/5) and [issue #6 — critique as evidence](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/issues/6), both created 2026-11-14. **Links:** P-01, P-02, P-03, P-05, P-06, P-07.

**Observed:** Organic exchanges raised two distinctions: a person's goals and constraints are not the same as their preferred conclusion; and reporting an unsatisfying behavior is not equivalent to proving the first explanation or proposed repair is right. **Interpretation:** Respect the human's experience, authority and preferences while assessing factual diagnoses and general-policy changes on their merits. **Alternatives:** Excessive independent critique can become defensiveness, performative disagreement, or refusal to honor an ordinary preference. **Evidence:** Abstracted, related collaboration observations; no claim of independent repeated trials. **Disposition:** Keep the issues as separate tentative candidates; existing SEED/INTEGRITY already cover much of the principle. No new seed edit or compulsory experiment. **Reassess if** the stance causes needless debate or factual quality still tracks the user's unsupported preference.

### 4. Notice meaningful evidence during work; reconcile later, without collecting everything

**Existing design proposal:** [PR #7 — cumulative evidence reconciliation](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/pull/7), proposed 2026-11-14; candidate label **OGM-CER-20261114**. **Links:** P-02/P-05, constrained by P-03/P-06.

**Observed:** A human collaborator identified the risk that a weekly retrospective could miss context that becomes unavailable, while a named hypothesis might bias future attention. **Interpretation:** Selectively recognize important corrections, surprises and counterexamples while working, then compare against actual prior evidence at a suitable milestone. Separate **attention**, **evidentiary confidence**, and **priority**. **Alternatives and failure modes:** A candidate index or extra seed rule might increase confirmation bias, record-keeping friction, privacy exposure, or needless architecture for ordinary users. Also a seed cannot create memory or authorized access. **Evidence:** A proposed design prompted by an observed gap, not demonstrated improvement. **Disposition:** PR #7 already covers this in detail, including an optional formal test. **Do not duplicate, merge automatically, or make its test mandatory.** Before adoption, decide whether its portable-seed addition has sufficient net value compared with retaining the mechanism only in an optional companion guide.
