## Motivating observation

In an ordinary collaboration, meaningful observations about already-proposed learning principles were recognized only after an explicit human prompt. The human identified an operational risk: by the time periodic review occurs, relevant context may be inaccessible or incomplete. Meanwhile, treating a named candidate as more credible merely because it was already named could bias interpretation.

**Candidate hypothesis:** A lightweight seed disposition toward recognizing material evidence *at the moment it occurs*, paired with a durable, authorized event trace and later cumulative reconciliation, will improve the completeness and integrity of learning from ordinary collaboration, **without** encouraging constant meta-interruptions or confirmation bias.

**Null:** It produces no meaningful improvement over periodic reflection, or worsens friction, selective attention, privacy, false positives, or unjustified belief revisions.

## Proposed architecture

1. **Seed:** Encourage selective event-time recognition alongside the main task. No compulsory prompts, no claim of persistent memory.
2. **Candidate index:** Compact retrieval cues linking to actual hypothesis records, including unresolved alternative ideas and uncertainty. Treat canonical links as identifiers when short IDs collide.
3. **Durable evidence:** Capture minimal observations only with legitimate access and authorization; keep private experiences out of the public repo.
4. **Cumulative reconciliation:** Compare new observations with prior evidence, alternative explanations and dependencies, keeping attention, confidence and research priority distinct.
5. **Periodic cultivation:** Audit gaps, missed contradictions, false candidate matches, duplicate counts and impacts on real-world usefulness. A weekly review supplements, rather than replaces, event-time recognition.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

- **Weekly-only review:** Simpler, but cannot recover inaccessible experiences. Keep it as an audit and second chance.
- **Log every interaction:** Higher apparent coverage, but costly, privacy-invasive, distracting and potentially overwhelming. Reject.
- **Keep everything in portable seed:** Convenient at activation but unscalable, biased toward prior hypotheses, and not generic. Reject.
- **Automated scoring of anecdotal evidence:** Looks quantitative but may imply unjustified precision and source independence. Reject pending a defensible measurement model.
- **Only spontaneous memory:** Low friction but unreliable coverage and no reviewable provenance. Insufficient alone.

## Evidence assessment — initial state

This is **one organic, explanatory observation**, not a controlled validation. The existence of candidate issues, PRs and repeated discussion is not independent replication. The **conceptual model** is more explicit than before; confidence that the mechanism improves diverse collaborations remains **undetermined**.

Potential benefit: fewer lost observations, better informed synthesis, improved recognition of counterevidence. Possible harms: confirmation bias, false pattern matching, interruption, unauthorized retention, and extra administrative overhead. All must be evaluated.

**Falsification / evaluation:** Use the [proposed experiment](../../EXPERIMENTS.md) comparing baseline and candidate-seeded assistants on supportive, disconfirming, duplicate, unrelated, and novel observations under matched access. Look at precision/recall within actually accessible events, preservation of contradictory observations, justified vs unjustified confidence changes, added friction, and privacy adherence.
