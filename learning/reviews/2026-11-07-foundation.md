## What emerged

The collaborators identified a possibility: a portable, optimally generic set of working principles might give many human–AI pairs a better **starting point** for developing their own effective collaboration. The aim is to share useful learning, not to market a clone of an existing assistant or impose its original personality.

The first implementation separates a **self-contained seed** from optional deeper guides:

- [SEED.md](../../SEED.md) — immediately usable collaboration instructions
- [GROWTH.md](../../GROWTH.md) — controlled adaptation, feedback and portability
- [INTEGRITY.md](../../INTEGRITY.md) — epistemic integrity, independence, boundaries and human agency
- [EXPERIMENTS.md](../../EXPERIMENTS.md) — informal evaluation approaches
- [LEARNING_MAP.md](../../LEARNING_MAP.md) — living conceptual relationships

The public [repository](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat) and its Git history document what was actually committed. This document does not claim model experiments or external research validation have been performed.

## Decisions

| ID | Decision | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OGM-20261107-01 | The minimal distribution artifact is one portable `SEED.md` | Low-friction adoption across assistants | Implemented in initial seed |
| OGM-20261107-02 | Personalize *to the new human*, do not clone the original assistant | Generality, user agency, privacy and honesty | Implemented in initial seed |
| OGM-20261107-03 | Seek independent thinking, curiosity, faithful challenge and honest uncertainty | Quality of reasoning is more important than polished agreement | Founding design preference; not empirically proven |
| OGM-20261107-04 | Preserve the evolution of ideas through a map, dated reviews, and traceable decisions | A changelog alone cannot tell us why or how understanding changed | Implemented in map and ledger scaffold |
| OGM-20261107-05 | Weekly cultivation should propose changes for review, not silently merge | Keeps the human responsible for approving normative changes | Governance policy; weekly task to be configured |
| OGM-20261107-06 | A review with no warranted change is still valuable | Avoids performative novelty and drift | Governance policy |

## Unknowns worth investigating

- Does a seed improve measurable results versus ordinary assistant instructions?
- Do different models interpret it reliably without drifting toward excessive agreement or verbosity?
- Which principles are generally useful versus local preferences?
- What changes persist across chats when no memory tool exists?
- How can evidence be collected ethically while avoiding publication of private user interactions?
