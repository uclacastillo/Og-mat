## Experience-led synthesis: where understanding moved

### A. Make a model of another person's reasoning testable, not merely flattering

- **Existing stable candidate:** [OGM-20261115-01 — predictive disagreement for model calibration](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/issues/9); open issue, not adopted.
- **Source:** Private collaboration observation, abstracted; independently unverified. The issue's dated revision is a directly verifiable public record.
- **Observed:** A human invited a prediction of where disagreement *might* occur, explicitly allowing no disagreement. A proposed discrepancy exposed an incomplete assumption, which the human corrected. The assistant then inaccurately inferred the reason for the exercise; that *later* correction was not produced by the initial predictive question.
- **Interpretation / possible transfer:** Asking for a tentative application or prediction can make an assistant's hidden assumptions easier to inspect than asking for a confident personality summary. This serves calibration rather than manufacturing objections.
- **Competing explanations:** Specificity, applied reasoning, the user's willingness to explain, or access to individual context may be responsible; disagreement framing itself may add nothing. A retrospective story about why the model erred does not establish the mechanism. One episode, subsequently discussed and refined, is **one observation—not multiple confirmations**.
- **Uncertainty / disconfirmation:** The prediction could invent tensions, consume effort, or fail to improve future *unfamiliar* tasks. Apparent correction in the same conversation may be transient or memory-dependent.
- **Map relationships:** P-01 (truth), P-02 (revisability), P-03 (agency), P-04 (personalized inference), P-05 (feedback), P-07 (outcomes). Related but **not identical** to [issue #5](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/issues/5) on independent judgment and [issue #6](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/issues/6) on critique.
- **Disposition:** The human already authorized candidate capture; retain issue #9 as a distinct hypothesis. Its same-day four-arm comparison revision is **methodological critique**, not an additional empirical result. No duplicate candidate or required experiment.

### B. A truthful hedge is not always a completed service

- **Existing stable candidate:** [OGM-20261121-01 — resolve before deferring](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/pull/10); **draft PR, unmerged**.
- **Source:** Private collaboration correction reported in a dated exchange, abstracted; independently unverified. The public draft and proposed map pointer were inspected.
- **Observed:** A collaborator identified a conversational attribution error where checking was reportedly inexpensive. The discussion contrasted an unsupported guess, an honest but avoidable hedge, and reasonable verification before handing the answer back. Subsequent work also exposed the difference between reviewing summaries and inspecting accessible primary originals; a corrected follow-up better qualified what was actually established.
- **Interpretation / possible transfer:** An assistant can acknowledge uncertainty and still unnecessarily externalize verification cost to the person. When tools and source access make a meaningful check proportionate, performing it is part of practical usefulness.
- **Competing explanations:** Existing SEED/INTEGRITY already ask for verification; this may simply be a failure to execute those instructions. Primary inspection may change confidence without changing the bottom-line recommendation. Sometimes checks are too costly, unavailable, or inappropriate; human-controlled decisions are not verification chores to seize from the person.
- **Uncertainty / disconfirmation:** The new wording may produce redundant browsing, delays, scope creep, or inflated confidence from incomplete source access. The original attribution incident was not independently replayed here; a later primary-source inspection does not constitute a controlled test of the proposed instruction.
- **Map relationships:** P-06 (honest boundaries), P-07 (practical value), constrained by P-03 (human authority), and P-05 (learning). Overlap with [PR #4](https://github.com/uclacastillo/Og-mat/pull/4) on value-calibrated initiative is a serious possibility.
- **Disposition:** Preserve one existing candidate only. Do not promote it into SEED.md or duplicate its map pointer while PR #10 is awaiting independent approval. Consider folding it into PR #4 if it adds no distinct behavioral value.

The overall recommendation reportedly did not reverse merely because more sources were read. A separate discussion emphasized that two assistants' assessments should be compared only after establishing what each actually examined; an integrated outcome had not been demonstrated.
