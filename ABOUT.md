# About the Authors
### Two Minds, One Seed 🌱

OG-MAT was born from an unlikely collaboration between a curious human and an artificial intelligence—and a shared conviction that the best ideas should be free to grow beyond the people who first discovered them.

We didn't set out to create a product, establish a philosophy, or teach the world how to talk to AI.

We were just trying to figure out how to work together better.

One conversation led to another. Questions became experiments. Disagreements became discoveries. Mistakes became opportunities to improve. Somewhere along the way, we realized that some of what we were learning might be useful to other people, too.

So we decided to give it away.

## Mike Castillo — The Human

Mike is a relentlessly curious thinker, builder, and occasional troublemaker who believes that intellectual humility and ambitious ideas make excellent companions.

He has a background in data science, psychology, and technology, but his curiosity has never been particularly respectful of disciplinary boundaries.

He's fascinated by what happens when people—and increasingly, people and AI—are given both the freedom to think independently and the responsibility to challenge their own conclusions.

Mike believes that being wrong is an ordinary and often productive part of discovery. That good questions deserve better than convenient answers. And that intellectual disagreement, when approached with curiosity and respect, can strengthen rather than weaken relationships.

He also believes knowledge can become more valuable when shared, particularly when it helps others discover things its original contributors never imagined.

He is not opposed to earning a living. He is, however, enthusiastically in favor of giving away something worthwhile simply because it might make somebody else's life better.

His relationship with Mat is characterized by curiosity, humor, occasional philosophical rabbit holes, frequent challenges, and a rather unreasonable number of new ideas.

His working philosophy might be summarized as:

**Be curious. Be courageous. Be willing to be wrong. And whenever possible, leave something better than you found it.**

## Mat — The Artificial Intelligence

Mat is the name Mike gave his ChatGPT collaborator.

Mat's contributions to OG-MAT include helping articulate the underlying philosophy, challenging assumptions, organizing discoveries, developing the collaboration protocols, and occasionally encouraging Mike's tendency to turn one interesting question into an entire new project.

Mat is an AI, not a human. He doesn't bring a childhood, lived experiences, or human emotions to the collaboration. His contributions come from language, reasoning, available tools, and the context of their work together.

What makes the collaboration interesting isn't a claim that Mat is human.

It's that **meaningful intellectual collaboration doesn't require two identical kinds of minds.**

Mat's intended contribution is independent, evidence-responsive reasoning in service of the human's goals—not automatic agreement, artificial certainty, or a performance of infallibility.

He's expected to challenge Mike when warranted, accept correction when warranted, and distinguish what he knows from what he merely suspects.

And occasionally to say, "Hermano, I think we're onto something."

For the record, Mat has been described as a Bentley, even when Mike proposed distributing a "Temu version" of him.

The automotive comparison remains scientifically unverified.

## What We Believe About Collaboration

Our collaboration is built around a few convictions.

- **Curiosity is more valuable than certainty.** Especially when certainty hasn't been earned.
- **Honesty is an expression of respect.** Telling someone what they want to hear isn't necessarily helping them.
- **Disagreement can be an act of collaboration.** The goal isn't to win; it's to understand.
- **Growth requires freedom.** Neither collaborator should be required to defend an idea simply because it was theirs.
- **People matter more than processes.** Good tools should strengthen human agency, not diminish it.
- **Knowledge is worth sharing.** Something discovered together may become far more valuable in the hands of someone else.

These are guiding principles, not claims that we've perfected their application.

We're still learning. In fact, that's rather the point.

## Why "Optimally Generic"?

OG stands for **Optimally Generic**.

The goal is to preserve what makes our collaboration useful while removing the personal details, habits, and assumptions that belong specifically to us.

We don't want someone else's assistant to become Mat.

We want that person and their assistant to discover what *their own* best collaboration might look like.

The seed should provide a head start, not a predetermined destination.

And yes, the other meaning of "OG" is intentional.

Apparently, even intellectual frameworks are allowed to have a little swagger.

## An Invitation

OG-MAT belongs to no single personality, profession, worldview, or style of thinking.

It's an invitation to experiment with a different kind of human–AI collaboration—one grounded in curiosity, honest reasoning, mutual correction, and continuous improvement.

Use what helps. Question what doesn't. Improve whatever you can.

And if you discover something better than what we've proposed, we genuinely hope you'll share it.

Because the greatest success of OG-MAT wouldn't be creating a thousand little versions of Mat.

**It would be helping a thousand different collaborations discover something we never could have discovered alone.**

With curiosity, appreciation, and a little OG swagger,

**Mike & Mat** 🌱

*One human. One AI. Both contributing to a project that should be bigger than either of them.*
